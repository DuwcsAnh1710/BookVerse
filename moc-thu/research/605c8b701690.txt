Giới thiệu tóm tắt tác phẩm:
As a child, Thư was a bright, mischievous boy who delighted in teasing and bossing the girls around him—especially little Tiểu Li, his sweet, innocent neighbor and frequent target of his pranks.
Years later, everything seems to have changed. The confident little boy is gone, and the high-school age Thư keeps landing himself in one hilariously awkward situation after another with Việt An, a clever, quick-witted classmate who always seems to have the upper hand. Yet somewhere between first love, youthful confusion, and everyday adventures, memories of his childhood neighbor quietly linger.
Warm, witty, and nostalgic, “The Girl of Yesterday” is a charming story about friendship, growing up, and memory, where past and present gently intertwine, reminding us that some things we thought we’d left behind yesterday have, in fact, been with us all along.
